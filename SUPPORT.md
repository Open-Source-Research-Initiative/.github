# Find help

Read the [OSRI research guide](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/osri/blob/main/START_HERE.md) or ask in [Q&A](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/.github/discussions/new?category=q-a).

Use [Ideas](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/.github/discussions/new?category=ideas) for research questions worth exploring. A rough question is enough.

For resources behind an access barrier, follow the [request guide](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/osri/blob/main/governance/resource-requests.md). Contact [Cole](mailto:cole.lyons@pennmedicine.upenn.edu) directly for confidential matters.
